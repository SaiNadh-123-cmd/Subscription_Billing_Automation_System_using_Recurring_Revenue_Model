Task 8: Subscription Lifecycle Operations Center

Direct API operations: Change Plan, Pause Subscription, Resume Subscription, and Cancel Subscription.

Change Plan

Switch subscription to an alternate active tier.

Pause Subscription

Temporarily halt billing. Allowed from trial or active states.

Resume Subscription

Reactivate subscription. Only permitted when currently paused.

Cancel Subscription

Moves to terminal cancelled state and completes pending cycles.
